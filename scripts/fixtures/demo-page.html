Every crew runs on a demo of the same routine: check in, hand off, write it down. The teams that keep their notes are the ones that keep their people. This page is a demo of that routine, written the way a real handbook reads.

A good handoff note names the demo site, the person who owns it, and the one thing that changed. Keep it to three lines and the next crew will read it.

Questions the demo raised

How long does a demo run? One shift, sometimes two. The demo ends when the notes are written.

Who reads the notes? The next crew, and Dana on Monday. Send corrections to corrections@northwind.example.
